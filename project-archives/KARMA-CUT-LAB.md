## What CUT LAB explored

KARMA CUT LAB was an experimental hairstyle, beard and face-preview experience. The direction evolved from a haircut simulator toward a more ambitious personalized visual studio.

### Design and development milestones

- **CUT LAB:** An interactive hairstyle preview and makeover concept.
- **CUT LAB Ultra:** A richer presentation with a 12-style selection grid and a more refined visual interface.
- **Photo Intelligence:** Experiments toward a single accurate style preview using user-supplied photographs.
- **Face Engine:** Experiments and plans for 3D facial modeling, hairstyle and beard previews.

These are project directions and prototype labels. This archive does not claim that all planned features were completed or validated.

## Creative principles

- Photorealistic results over generic beauty-filter effects.
- Preserve a subject's recognizability, proportions and facial character.
- Make the preview responsive, understandable and optional to use.
- Avoid keeping personal photographs in a public source repository.
- Treat uploads as sensitive data and require clear consent.
